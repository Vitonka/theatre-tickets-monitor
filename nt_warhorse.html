About the show

The last chance to see the global phenomenon gallop home to the National Theatre

At the outbreak of the First World War, young Albert’s beloved horse Joey is sold to the cavalry and shipped to France. Too young to enlist, Albert refuses to forget him, embarking on an extraordinary journey from the fields of rural Devon to the trenches of wartime France – determined to bring Joey home.

Based on Michael Morpurgo’s beloved novel, War Horse is a timeless story of love, courage and friendship, brought to life by astonishing life-sized horses from Handspring Puppet Company and a stirring musical score.

Following a sell-out national tour, the Tony® Award–winning global sensation returns to where it all began, nearly two decades after its debut at the National Theatre.

 

Book official War Horse tickets for the Tony Award-winning global sensation, based on Michael Morpurgo’s beloved novel. Playing in the Olivier Theatre at the National Theatre in London from Saturday 16 May to Thursday 30 July 2026, with performances this summer, it’s an unforgettable family day out.

Assisted performances

The National Theatre offers a range of accessible theatre performances for War Horse, to support Deaf, disabled and neurodivergent audiences, as well as anyone who may benefit from additional access support when attending this stage production in London.

Explore accessible performances for War Horse below:

Captioned

Captioned performances have text on screens so that deaf and hard of hearing, or anyone that would like to read along, can also enjoy the performance.
Tuesday 28 July – 7.30pm
Book Tickets for War Horse

BSL Interpreted

There will be a trained British Signed Language (BSL) interpreter, usually standing to one side of the stage, interpreting the script used by the performers at the same time as it is being performed.
Wednesday 22 July – 7.30pm
Book Tickets for War Horse

Audio described

Audio description is a live commentary interspersed with the actors' dialogue. This is relayed via a headset.
Friday 24 July – 7.30pm
Book Tickets for War Horse

No Chilled performance available.

No Dementia-friendly performances available.

No Relaxed performance available.

The interpreter for the BSL performance will be Donna Ruane.

Audio-described performances are preceded by a Touch Tour, 90 minutes before the performance start time. Call the Access line on 020 7452 3961 to book Touch Tours.

The Audio describers will be Eleanor Margolies and Tony McBride.
